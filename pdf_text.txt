Idea & Proposed Solution
IDEA TITLE
Smart Solar Cold Storage System
PROPOSED SOLUTION
An IoT-enabled, solar-powered cold storage unit designed for small-scale farmers to preserve produce quality and reduce post-
harvest losses
DETAILED EXPLANATION
The system combines passive insulation with active Peltier cooling, powered entirely by solar energy with battery backup. Real-
time monitoring via ESP32 tracks temperature, humidity, and door status, enabling automatic cooling control and remote alerts.
HOW IT ADDRESSES THE PROBLEM
Eliminates dependency on distant cold chain facilities, reduces spoilage by 40-60%, enables farmers to store produce longer and 
sell at better market prices
INNOVATION & UNIQUENESS
First solar-powered, IoT-enabled cold storage designed specifically for 20-40L farm-level use; combines affordability (₹25,000) 
with smart monitoring; DC-powered architecture for maximum efficiency
